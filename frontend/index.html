About FactGuard

FactGuard is an open-source, automated fact-checking pipeline. We understand that in the modern era of deepfakes and AI-generated hallucinations, verifying the truth requires tools that act faster than disinformation spreads. FactGuard transcribes your media and flags every claim with a probability score.

Misinformation rapidly propagates through visual and auditory media where it escapes traditional text-based fact checkers. FactGuard bridges this gap, giving journalists, researchers, and general citizens the enterprise-grade power to drag-and-drop a video and receive instant truth scores. By cross-referencing isolated statements through SerpAPI against millions of databases, FactGuard provides a transparent line of credible reality.

How to Use FactGuard

1️⃣

Upload Media

Navigate to your Dashboard and drag-and-drop any MP4, MP3, or WAV file into the verification zone (Max 500MB).

2️⃣

Automated Analysis

Click 'Verify'. The system utilizes Groq's high-speed Whisper AI to instantly extract audio and transcribe it, piping the text directly into the Llama LLM for claim separation.

3️⃣

Review Results

A percentage credibility score is ultimately rendered. You can view distinct flags for True, False, or Partially True statements on your dashboard.

Help & FAQ

Why did my upload fail?

Files over 500MB or unsupported codecs might fail. Try converting your file to standard MP4 or MP3 formats before uploading. Make sure your network does not block localhost upload streams.

How is the accuracy score computed?

The overarching score is an average of the individual certainty scores produced by the LLM fact-checking pipeline for each distinct claim isolated from the transcript.

Are my videos stored permanently?

No. Your files are temporarily stored for extraction within the /uploads/ directory and deleted immediately after processing via FFmpeg.

Understanding the architecture.

How it Works

FactGuard utilizes Groq's high-speed Whisper AI to transcribe audio locally or remotely in milliseconds. We then parse the text using Llama to isolate factual claims. Each claim is cross-referenced with modern search engines via SerpAPI to triangulate truth scores.


Our Mission

In an era of deepfakes and AI-generated disinformation, fact-checking needs to be accessible, immediate, and transparent. We've built FactGuard to serve journalists, analysts, and everyday consumers.
